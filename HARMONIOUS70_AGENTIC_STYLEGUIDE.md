### Accessibility & Warm Technology Vectors
This architecture actively practices **Warm Technology**—designing for human dignity, clarity, and non-stigmatizing inclusion. 

To ensure the space remains highly navigable for **dyslexic and ADHD readers**:
- **Contrast over Aesthetics:** Never sacrifice legibility for mood. The `day` mode explicitly uses high-contrast ink (`#11110f`) and deeper signal colors (`#7d5e12`) to prevent visual bleed on light backgrounds.
- **Typographic Stability:** Avoid long blocks of italic text. Use sans-serif foundations (`DM Sans` or `STEAM SANS`) with `font-style: normal` as the default for all `.h70-body` copy.
- **Cognitive Clarity & Spacing:** Enforce a maximum line width of `65ch`, left-aligned text, and a minimum line height of `1.75`. Generous letter spacing (`0.015em`) prevents visual crowding.
- **Sensory Agency:** Motion should be smooth and predictable. Avoid sudden flashes. The `muralist` operates on a slow 12-second cycle, anchoring the nervous system rather than disrupting it.
