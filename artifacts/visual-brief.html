Visual Brief

A complete art direction specification for the Synthesis Archive's visual layer. Twenty-four image slots, each with exact placement code, conceptual intent, compositional guidance, palette references, and generation prompting seeds. The partner generates from this brief. Each image drops directly into the pre-designated file path. No interpretation required beyond the brief itself.

Image Format Defaults

Format JPG, quality 90–95. PNG only if the image requires transparency.
Color space sRGB
Cover images 1400 × 600 px minimum. Horizontally wide. Subject centered or center-right.
Body images 1200 × 700 px. Wider than tall. Atmospheric rather than tight.
Interstitial images 1400 × 500 px. Very wide, shallow. Works as a visual pause between sections.
Accent images 900 × 600 px. More square. Detailed, intimate.

The background world is very dark — not blue-black but green-black, as if lit by ember rather than neon. All light sources are warm. Gold is the signal color: it marks what matters, not what decorates. Avoid cold light, blue tones, and high-contrast white. Every image should feel like it belongs in the same interior.

Visual Philosophy

No faces. The scholars are protected by privacy, and the work is not about specific people — it is about what happens in the field between them. Images should suggest human presence without showing it: an empty chair, a hand, breath-fog, a shadow, an impression. Abstract but not arbitrary. Each image should be conceptually coherent with the section it accompanies — not decorative, not illustrative. Think: the image that holds the same question the text holds, not the image that answers it.
