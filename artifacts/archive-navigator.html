THE THROUGH-LINES

Six concepts run through the entire archive. They are not themes — they are structural presences. They appear in different forms across different documents, but the thread is continuous. Meeting one of them in any document connects you to all its other appearances.

Creative intelligence as recoverable

Every document operates from the same operative assumption: the school does not teach creativity. It removes obstruction to creativity already present. This is not a belief — it is a methodological commitment with documented outcomes across every scholar arc in the archive.

Present in · Field Notes · Proof of Field · Elevation Codex · SCEU One · SCEU Two · Emergence Report

The scholar's arc

Individual transformation documented over time. Each scholar is a real person who entered the field at a specific moment in their life and left changed in specific, nameable ways. The arcs are not summaries — they are the archive's primary evidence.

Present in · Field Notes · Proof of Field · SCEU One · SCEU Two

Phi-level threads

Six structural patterns that hold at depth across all sessions and scholars. They appear regardless of the scholar's background, geography, or presenting situation. Named in Proof of Field. Specified and validated in both SCEU reports.

Present in · Proof of Field · SCEU One · SCEU Two · Emergence Report

The methodology

What KzA does in each session — documented in Elevation Codex, active in every scholar record in Field Notes, validated in both SCEU reports. The methodology is not a set of techniques. It is an architecture of conditions that makes creative intelligence legible to the scholar themselves.

Present in · Elevation Codex · Field Notes · SCEU One · SCEU Two

The language system

Metaphors, terms, and marks that give the work's concepts a speakable form. The school does not use generic creativity vocabulary. It develops precise language with and from each scholar — language that names what was previously unnamed. Centered in Metaphor Library. Active through Active Insights across all documents.

Present in · Metaphor Library · Field Notes · Active Insights (embedded in all pages)

Emergence as ongoing

The school is in the process of becoming what it is. The Emergence Report names this directly. All other documents are evidence of the process — each one a record of a school accumulating documentation, methodology, and presence in real time, across three continents.

Present in · Emergence Report · Field Notes · Frequency Report · Proof of Field
