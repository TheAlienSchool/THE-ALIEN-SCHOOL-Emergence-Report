From the Field · Forli, Fan, Qi & Yartsev · Nature 2025
5 Findings from Egyptian Fruit Bats
The bat's hippocampus runs ahead of its body. The decoded position swept forward cyclically with every wing-beat. The bat's cognitive map was always projecting into the near future. Recognition precedes arrival.
Replay occurs at remote locations. During rest, hippocampal neurons replayed flight trajectories from spatially distant locations. The bat resting on one wall replays routes from the other side. PING™ operates from anywhere in the field.
Replay duration is constant. Whether a 2.7m or 20m route, replay takes approximately the same time (~350ms). The circuit has a fixed duration. What changes is information density.
Echolocation suppresses the internal forward sweep. Emission and reception alternate. The bat cannot fully emit and receive simultaneously. The still between the wing-beats is where reception lives.
The wing-beat is the clock. No theta oscillations during flight. The body's own rhythm organizes hippocampal sequences. The behavior organizes the knowing.
